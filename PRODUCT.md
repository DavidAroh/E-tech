# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Mid-market and enterprise decision-makers (CROs, CTOs, digital transformation leads, board-level stakeholders) evaluating AI adoption and cybersecurity posture. They arrive under time pressure, often in regulated or high-stakes sectors (finance, healthcare, energy, government), and need a consultancy that signals discretion, competence, and governance maturity rather than product marketing energy.

## Product Purpose

Etela Technologies (E-Tech) is a boutique AI Advisory & Cybersecurity consulting firm. The primary surface is a marketing site that positions E-Tech as the bridge between AI adoption and security/governance, converts visitors into consultation bookings, and supports future content routes (insights, careers, team). Success is qualified consultation requests and brand trust, not signup freemium metrics.

## Brand Personality

Luxurious, minimal, corporate-serious. Tagline: *Rise. Defend. Overcome.* Think boutique consultancy boardroom, not SaaS landing page. Voice is measured, discreet, executive-ready; never playful, neon, or startup-hype.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-startup landing pages (glowing brains, robot hands, mesh gradients, neon purple glows)
- Playful SaaS templates (bouncy motion, emoji, cartoon illustrations)
- Identical three-column feature card walls with thick icons
- Pure light "cream agency" portfolios and loud gradient-text heroes
- Consumer app energy or gamer/cyberpunk aesthetics

## Design Principles

1. **Boardroom first** - Every surface should feel safe to open in a C-suite meeting: restrained, legible, no spectacle for its own sake.
2. **Depth without noise** - Alternate dark surfaces (black/cocoa) for rhythm; use glass and purple accent sparingly and with intent.
3. **Governed ambition** - AI and security themes expressed through abstract structure (nodes, shield motif), never sci-fi cliché.
4. **One light moment for paperwork** - The consultation form is the sole light panel; it signals premium intake and readability.
5. **Expand without rework** - Nav and IA leave room for insights, careers, team, portal, and legal pages without redesigning the shell.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA: semantic landmarks, visible focus rings in brand purple-light, labeled form fields with errors, alt text on illustrations, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored. Dark-first theme (no light/dark toggle required for v1). Touch targets and keyboard paths for nav, search modal, FAQ, and forms are first-class.
